Privacy Policy.

LAST REVISED · JULY 7, 2026

Burrows Applications ("we", "us") builds mobile applications and this website, burrowsapps.com (the "Site"). This policy explains what information the Site and our published apps (the "Apps") handle, and how. The short version: we collect as little as possible, and we don't sell personal information.

What the Site collects

  • Analytics. The Site uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand traffic — pages viewed, approximate location, browser and device type. These services set cookies and are governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Contact form. If you use the contact form, the name, email address, and message you enter are delivered to our inbox through a Cloudflare Worker. We use them only to respond to you — they are not added to any mailing list or shared with anyone else.
  • Fonts. Pages load the Instrument Sans and IBM Plex Mono typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, Google receives your IP address and browser information; this is governed by Google's privacy policy.

What the Apps collect

Our Android apps are designed to run entirely on your device. They don't require an account and don't collect personal information. Where an individual app has additional data practices, they are disclosed in that app's Google Play listing and data-safety section.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal information.
  • We don't run third-party advertising on the Site.
  • We don't ask for more data than a feature needs.

Retention

Messages sent through the contact form are kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings.

Third-party links

The Site links to third-party services such as LinkedIn and X / Twitter. This policy doesn't extend to those services — their own policies apply.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version always lives at burrowsapps.com/privacy.html with the revision date shown above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or our privacy practices? Email contact@burrowsapps.com.
